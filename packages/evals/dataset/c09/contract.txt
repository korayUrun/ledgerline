ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Bluebird Presents Inc. ("Promoter"), operating Riverside Ballroom (the "Venue"), and The Silver Satellites ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on October 21, 2026, with a set of not less than 76 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of US $250.00 and a hot meal for up to 9 persons.

3. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 35 miles of the Venue during the 70 days before the show date.

4. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at Thirty Dollars (US $30.00) for GA. A service charge of $4 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

5. Show Expenses
Promoter is responsible for all show costs. Artist's compensation is not reduced by any expense.

6. Artist Fee
6.1 Promoter shall pay Artist a flat guarantee of Two Thousand Five Hundred Dollars (US $2,500.00) for the Engagement. This amount is not subject to any deduction or box office split.
6.2 Only the single highest bonus level reached is payable: 350 or more paid tickets: US $750.00; 450 or more paid tickets: US $1,000.00.
6.3 Complimentary tickets do not count toward any bonus.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Bluebird Presents Inc.
For Artist: The Silver Satellites
