ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Northgate Live LLC ("Promoter"), operating The Foundry (the "Venue"), and The Hollow Harbors ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on May 9, 2026, with a set of not less than 69 minutes. Doors open at 7:30 p.m.

2. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 65 miles of the Venue during the 90 days before the show date.

3. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at US $40.00 for GA and $40 for Balcony. A service charge of $5 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

4. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than One Million Dollars (US $1,000,000.00) per occurrence.

5. Expenses
All production and operating costs of the Engagement are borne by Promoter. No expenses shall be deducted from Artist's compensation.

6. Compensation
6.1 Promoter shall pay Artist a flat guarantee of US $17,250.00 for the Engagement. This amount is not subject to any deduction or box office split.
6.2 A deposit of $8,625.00 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

7. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of $175.00 and a hot meal for up to 9 persons.

8. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Northgate Live LLC
For Artist: The Hollow Harbors
