ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Northgate Live LLC ("Promoter"), operating Riverside Ballroom (the "Venue"), and The Paper Foxes ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on March 8, 2026, with a set of not less than 64 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 80% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 20% as a merchandise fee.

3. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than US $1,000,000.00 per occurrence.

4. Show Expenses
Promoter is responsible for all show costs. Artist's compensation is not reduced by any expense.

5. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 85 miles of the Venue during the 70 days before the show date.

6. Compensation
6.1 In full consideration of the performance, Promoter agrees to pay Artist a fixed fee of $8,500.00 (the "Guarantee"). Artist shall not share in box office receipts.
6.2 Artist shall earn the attendance bonus for the highest level reached only; bonuses are not cumulative: 600 or more paid tickets: $500.00.
6.3 Complimentary tickets do not count toward any bonus.
6.4 A deposit of $4,250.00 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

7. Cancellation
If Promoter cancels the Engagement less than 39 days before the show date for any reason other than force majeure, Promoter shall pay Artist a cancellation fee of US $7,500.00.

8. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Northgate Live LLC
For Artist: The Paper Foxes
