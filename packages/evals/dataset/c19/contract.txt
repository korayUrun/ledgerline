ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Bluebird Presents Inc. ("Promoter"), operating Union Stage (the "Venue"), and The Paper Engines ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on September 24, 2026, with a set of not less than 73 minutes. Doors open at 7:30 p.m.

2. Ticket Bonus
Artist shall earn the attendance bonus for the highest level reached only; bonuses are not cumulative: 400 or more paid tickets: US $250.00; 550 or more paid tickets: Five Hundred Dollars (US $500.00).
Complimentary tickets do not count toward any bonus.

3. Show Expenses
Allowable Expenses are limited to equipment rental, catering, security personnel, house staffing, production (sound, lights and staging) and advertising. No other cost may be deducted.
Allowable catering expenses shall not exceed Two Thousand Dollars (US $2,000.00).
There is no overall limit on Allowable Expenses.
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

4. Artist Fee
4.1 Artist shall receive a guarantee of $15,750 versus 87.5% of Net Box Office Receipts after deduction of Allowable Expenses, whichever is greater.
4.2 A deposit of $7,875 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

5. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of One Hundred Seventy-Five Dollars (US $175.00) and a hot meal for up to 11 persons.

6. Cancellation
If Promoter cancels the Engagement less than 42 days before the show date for any reason other than force majeure, Promoter shall pay Artist a cancellation fee of Three Thousand Five Hundred Dollars (US $3,500.00).

7. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000.00 per occurrence.

8. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Bluebird Presents Inc.
For Artist: The Paper Engines
