ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Northgate Live LLC ("Promoter"), operating The Foundry (the "Venue"), and The Silver Engines ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on June 20, 2026, with a set of not less than 76 minutes. Doors open at 8:00 p.m.

2. Expenses
Promoter is responsible for all show costs. Artist's compensation is not reduced by any expense.

3. Compensation
Artist shall receive 52.5% of Net Box Office Receipts. No guarantee applies to this Engagement.

4. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 55 miles of the Venue during the 70 days before the show date.

5. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at $30 for GA and $30.00 for Balcony. A service charge of $3.00 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

6. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 90% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 10% as a merchandise fee.

7. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000.00 per occurrence.

8. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Northgate Live LLC
For Artist: The Silver Engines
