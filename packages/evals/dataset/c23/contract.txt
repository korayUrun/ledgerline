ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Bluebird Presents Inc. ("Promoter"), operating The Foundry (the "Venue"), and The Midnight Pines ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on November 11, 2026, with a set of not less than 76 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence.

3. Cancellation
If Promoter cancels the Engagement less than 43 days before the show date for any reason other than force majeure, Promoter shall pay Artist a cancellation fee of $4,000.

4. Artist Fee
4.1 Promoter shall pay Artist the greater of (a) Sixteen Thousand Dollars (US $16,000.00) (the "Guarantee") or (b) 82.5% of net box office receipts remaining after Allowable Expenses have been deducted.
4.2 A deposit of US $8,000.00 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

5. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at $50.00 for GA and Fifty Dollars (US $50.00) for Balcony. A service charge of $3.00 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

6. Expenses
The following costs are Allowable Expenses and may be deducted before Artist's share is calculated: production costs, catering, security, house staffing, rented equipment and advertising.
Allowable production costs expenses shall not exceed $1,500.
Allowable security expenses shall not exceed $1,250.
In no event shall Allowable Expenses exceed $7,000 in the aggregate.
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Bluebird Presents Inc.
For Artist: The Midnight Pines
