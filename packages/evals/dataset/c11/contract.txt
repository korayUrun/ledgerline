ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Northgate Live LLC ("Promoter"), operating The Granary (the "Venue"), and The Golden Rivers ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on June 23, 2026, with a set of not less than 65 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of $350 and a hot meal for up to 4 persons.

3. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than Two Million Dollars (US $2,000,000.00) per occurrence.

4. Allowable Expenses
The following costs are Allowable Expenses and may be deducted before Artist's share is calculated: production (sound, lights and staging), venue staff (ushers and box office) and equipment rental.
Equipment rental costs are capped at $1,250.00.
In no event shall Allowable Expenses exceed US $3,000.00 in the aggregate.
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

5. Payment to Artist
Artist shall receive a guarantee of $7,750.00 versus 72.5% of Net Box Office Receipts after deduction of Allowable Expenses, whichever is greater.

6. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 85 miles of the Venue during the 70 days before the show date.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Northgate Live LLC
For Artist: The Golden Rivers
