ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating Riverside Ballroom (the "Venue"), and The Golden Pines ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on May 7, 2026, with a set of not less than 76 minutes. Doors open at 8:00 p.m.

2. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of Five Hundred Seventy-Five Dollars (US $575.00) and a hot meal for up to 7 persons.

3. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 25 miles of the Venue during the 90 days before the show date.

4. Expenses
Allowable Expenses are limited to production (sound, lights and staging), equipment rental, security and catering. No other cost may be deducted.
There is no overall limit on Allowable Expenses.
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

5. Artist Fee
5.1 Compensation is US $23,500.00 (the "Guarantee") plus a back-end share: after Promoter has recovered Allowable Expenses, the Guarantee and a Promoter Profit equal to ten percent (10%) of Allowable Expenses plus the Guarantee, Artist shall receive seventy-five percent (75%) of all remaining net box office receipts.
5.2 A deposit of $11,750.00 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

6. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Golden Pines
