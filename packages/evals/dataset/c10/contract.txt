ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Northgate Live LLC ("Promoter"), operating The Foundry (the "Venue"), and The Paper Harbors ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on September 18, 2026, with a set of not less than 89 minutes. Doors open at 8:00 p.m.

2. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of Two Hundred Fifty Dollars (US $250.00) and a hot meal for up to 8 persons.

3. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 35 miles of the Venue during the 30 days before the show date.

4. Expenses
Promoter is responsible for all show costs. Artist's compensation is not reduced by any expense.

5. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 70% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 30% as a merchandise fee.

6. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than US $2,000,000.00 per occurrence.

7. Artist Fee
7.1 As compensation, Promoter shall pay Artist an amount equal to eighty percent (80%) of net box office receipts, meaning gross ticket receipts less admission taxes and ticketing fees. There is no minimum guarantee.
7.2 Only the single highest bonus level reached is payable: upon the sale of 700 paid tickets, a bonus of US $500.00; upon the sale of 750 paid tickets, a bonus of $1,000.
7.3 Complimentary tickets do not count toward any bonus.

8. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Northgate Live LLC
For Artist: The Paper Harbors
