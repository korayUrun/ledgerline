ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating The Granary (the "Venue"), and The Paper Satellites ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on April 27, 2026, with a set of not less than 87 minutes. Doors open at 8:00 p.m.

2. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence.

3. Cancellation
If Promoter cancels the Engagement less than 15 days before the show date for any reason other than force majeure, Promoter shall pay Artist a cancellation fee of Seven Thousand Dollars (US $7,000.00).

4. Artist Fee
4.1 Artist shall receive a guarantee of US $9,500.00, plus seventy percent (70%) of Net Box Office Receipts in excess of the Split Point. The Split Point equals Allowable Expenses plus the Guarantee plus a promoter profit of 15% of the sum of Allowable Expenses and the Guarantee.
4.2 A deposit of US $4,750.00 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

5. Expenses
Allowable Expenses are limited to house staffing, production costs and equipment rental. No other cost may be deducted.
Allowable house staffing expenses shall not exceed $750.00.
In no event shall Allowable Expenses exceed $5,000 in the aggregate.
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

6. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 95 miles of the Venue during the 70 days before the show date.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Paper Satellites
