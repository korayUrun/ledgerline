ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating Union Stage (the "Venue"), and The Northern Satellites ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on December 25, 2026, with a set of not less than 90 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of $575 and a hot meal for up to 12 persons.

3. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 95 miles of the Venue during the 70 days before the show date.

4. Show Expenses
All production and operating costs of the Engagement are borne by Promoter. No expenses shall be deducted from Artist's compensation.

5. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 70% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 30% as a merchandise fee.

6. Artist Fee
Artist shall receive 82.5% of Net Box Office Receipts. No guarantee applies to this Engagement.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Northern Satellites
