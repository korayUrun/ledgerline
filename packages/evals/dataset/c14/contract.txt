ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating Hall 9 (the "Venue"), and The Midnight Satellites ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on January 6, 2026, with a set of not less than 73 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Cancellation
If Promoter cancels the Engagement less than 36 days before the show date for any reason other than force majeure, Promoter shall pay Artist a cancellation fee of $4,500.

3. Payment to Artist
3.1 As compensation, Promoter shall pay Artist an amount equal to 72.5% of net box office receipts, meaning gross ticket receipts less admission taxes and ticketing fees. There is no minimum guarantee.
3.2 Artist shall earn the following attendance bonuses. Bonuses are cumulative: each bonus reached is paid in addition to the others: 450 or more paid tickets: US $250.00.
3.3 Complimentary tickets do not count toward any bonus.

4. Allowable Expenses
Promoter is responsible for all show costs. Artist's compensation is not reduced by any expense.

5. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of US $400.00 and a hot meal for up to 4 persons.

6. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Midnight Satellites
