ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating Riverside Ballroom (the "Venue"), and The Velvet Pines ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on March 6, 2026, with a set of not less than 89 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at $40.00 for GA and $100 for VIP. A service charge of US $6.00 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

3. Compensation
3.1 As compensation, Promoter shall pay Artist an amount equal to 50% of net box office receipts, meaning gross ticket receipts less admission taxes and ticketing fees. There is no minimum guarantee.
3.2 Only the single highest bonus level reached is payable: upon the sale of 550 paid tickets, a bonus of US $750.00; upon the sale of 850 paid tickets, a bonus of US $1,250.00.
3.3 Complimentary tickets do not count toward any bonus.

4. Cancellation
If Promoter cancels the Engagement less than 33 days before the show date for any reason other than force majeure, Promoter shall pay Artist a cancellation fee of Nine Thousand Five Hundred Dollars (US $9,500.00).

5. Expenses
Promoter is responsible for all show costs. Artist's compensation is not reduced by any expense.

6. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Velvet Pines
