ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Bluebird Presents Inc. ("Promoter"), operating Union Stage (the "Venue"), and The Paper Foxes ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on April 16, 2026, with a set of not less than 71 minutes. Doors open at 7:30 p.m.

2. Compensation
2.1 Promoter shall pay Artist a flat guarantee of $39,500.68 for the Engagement. This amount is not subject to any deduction or box office split.
2.2 A deposit of US $19,750.34 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

3. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 75% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 25% as a merchandise fee.

4. Show Expenses
Promoter is responsible for all show costs. Artist's compensation is not reduced by any expense.

5. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than $2,000,000 per occurrence.

6. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Bluebird Presents Inc.
For Artist: The Paper Foxes
