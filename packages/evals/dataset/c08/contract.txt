ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Northgate Live LLC ("Promoter"), operating Hall 9 (the "Venue"), and The Velvet Harbors ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on November 7, 2026, with a set of not less than 84 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Cancellation
If Promoter cancels the Engagement less than 20 days before the show date for any reason other than force majeure, Promoter shall pay Artist a cancellation fee of $7,000.00.

3. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 85% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 15% as a merchandise fee.

4. Artist Fee
4.1 Artist shall receive a guarantee of Eight Thousand Five Hundred Dollars (US $8,500.00), plus seventy percent (70%) of Net Box Office Receipts in excess of the Split Point. The Split Point equals Allowable Expenses plus the Guarantee plus a promoter profit of fifteen percent (15%) of the sum of Allowable Expenses and the Guarantee.
4.2 A deposit of $4,250.00 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

5. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of $525.00 and a hot meal for up to 5 persons.

6. Allowable Expenses
Allowable Expenses are limited to house staffing, equipment rental, security personnel, advertising, catering and production costs. No other cost may be deducted.
Total Allowable Expenses shall not exceed US $3,000.00.
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

8. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.
