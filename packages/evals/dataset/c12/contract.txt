ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Bluebird Presents Inc. ("Promoter"), operating The Granary (the "Venue"), and The Golden Satellites ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on December 19, 2026, with a set of not less than 83 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at Thirty Dollars (US $30.00) for GA and US $85.00 for VIP. A service charge of $4.00 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

3. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than One Million Dollars (US $1,000,000.00) per occurrence.

4. Payment to Artist
4.1 Compensation is Seven Thousand Two Hundred Fifty Dollars (US $7,250.00) (the "Guarantee") plus a back-end share: after Promoter has recovered Allowable Expenses, the Guarantee and a Promoter Profit equal to 20% of Allowable Expenses plus the Guarantee, Artist shall receive eighty percent (80%) of all remaining net box office receipts.
4.2 A deposit of Three Thousand Six Hundred Twenty-Five Dollars (US $3,625.00) is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

5. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 80% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 20% as a merchandise fee.

6. Show Expenses
Allowable Expenses are limited to security, advertising and marketing and venue staff (ushers and box office). No other cost may be deducted.
Advertising and marketing costs are capped at $1,250.
Allowable security expenses shall not exceed US $500.00.
Total Allowable Expenses shall not exceed Eleven Thousand Five Hundred Dollars (US $11,500.00).
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Bluebird Presents Inc.
For Artist: The Golden Satellites
