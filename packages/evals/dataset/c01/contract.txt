ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Bluebird Presents Inc. ("Promoter"), operating Hall 9 (the "Venue"), and The Midnight Pines ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on October 24, 2026, with a set of not less than 68 minutes. Doors open at 7:30 p.m.

2. Cancellation
If Promoter cancels the Engagement less than 37 days before the show date for any reason other than force majeure, Promoter shall pay Artist a cancellation fee of US $2,000.00.

3. Show Expenses
Promoter is responsible for all show costs. Artist's compensation is not reduced by any expense.

4. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 80% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 20% as a merchandise fee.

5. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence.

6. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of US $600.00 and a hot meal for up to 12 persons.

7. Compensation
7.1 In full consideration of the performance, Promoter agrees to pay Artist a fixed fee of US $20,500.00 (the "Guarantee"). Artist shall not share in box office receipts.
7.2 A deposit of $10,250.00 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

8. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of New York. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Bluebird Presents Inc.
For Artist: The Midnight Pines
