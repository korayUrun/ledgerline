ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Bluebird Presents Inc. ("Promoter"), operating Riverside Ballroom (the "Venue"), and The Paper Harbors ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on June 15, 2026, with a set of not less than 89 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Artist Fee
2.1 Promoter shall pay Artist the greater of (a) Five Thousand Five Hundred Dollars (US $5,500.00) (the "Guarantee") or (b) seventy percent (70%) of net box office receipts remaining after Allowable Expenses have been deducted.
2.2 A deposit of $2,750.00 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

3. Allowable Expenses
The following costs are Allowable Expenses and may be deducted before Artist's share is calculated: house staffing, catering, security and equipment rental.
Equipment rental costs are capped at $1,750.00.
In no event shall Allowable Expenses exceed $11,000.00 in the aggregate.
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

4. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at Thirty-Five Dollars (US $35.00) for GA and US $35.00 for Balcony. A service charge of $6 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

5. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 70% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 30% as a merchandise fee.

6. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of $150.00 and a hot meal for up to 11 persons.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Bluebird Presents Inc.
For Artist: The Paper Harbors
