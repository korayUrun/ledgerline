ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating The Granary (the "Venue"), and The Northern Rivers ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on December 28, 2026, with a set of not less than 87 minutes. Doors open at 8:00 p.m.

2. Expenses
All production and operating costs of the Engagement are borne by Promoter. No expenses shall be deducted from Artist's compensation.

3. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of US $175.00 and a hot meal for up to 12 persons.

4. Payment to Artist
4.1 Promoter shall pay Artist a flat guarantee of Twenty-Eight Thousand Two Hundred Fifty Dollars (US $28,250.00) for the Engagement. This amount is not subject to any deduction or box office split.
4.2 A deposit of $14,125 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

5. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 70% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 30% as a merchandise fee.

6. Attendance Bonus
The following bonuses apply, and every bonus level reached is payable: upon the sale of 650 paid tickets, a bonus of US $750.00; upon the sale of 850 paid tickets, a bonus of One Thousand Dollars (US $1,000.00); upon the sale of 1,000 paid tickets, a bonus of Two Thousand Dollars (US $2,000.00).
Complimentary tickets do not count toward any bonus.

7. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at US $50.00 for GA, $75.00 for VIP and $50 for Balcony. A service charge of $6 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

8. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 80 miles of the Venue during the 70 days before the show date.

9. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Northern Rivers
