ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating The Foundry (the "Venue"), and The Golden Pines ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on February 23, 2026, with a set of not less than 62 minutes. Doors open at 7:30 p.m.

2. Allowable Expenses
The following costs are Allowable Expenses and may be deducted before Artist's share is calculated: venue staff (ushers and box office), rented equipment, security personnel, advertising and marketing, catering and production (sound, lights and staging).
Advertising and marketing costs are capped at One Thousand Five Hundred Dollars (US $1,500.00).
Allowable catering expenses shall not exceed US $1,250.00.
In no event shall Allowable Expenses exceed US $7,000.00 in the aggregate.
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

3. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 85% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 15% as a merchandise fee.

4. Cancellation
If Promoter cancels the Engagement less than 18 days before the show date for any reason other than force majeure, Promoter shall pay Artist a cancellation fee of $7,000.

5. Hospitality
Promoter shall provide a hospitality buyout of $325.00 and a hot meal for up to 12 persons.

6. Payment to Artist
6.1 Artist shall receive a guarantee of $10,000.13 versus 90% of Net Box Office Receipts after deduction of Allowable Expenses, whichever is greater.
6.2 Artist shall earn the following attendance bonuses. Bonuses are cumulative: each bonus reached is paid in addition to the others: 500 or more paid tickets: US $500.00; upon the sale of 650 paid tickets, a bonus of US $1,500.00.
6.3 Complimentary tickets do not count toward any bonus.
6.4 A deposit of US $5,000.07 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Golden Pines
