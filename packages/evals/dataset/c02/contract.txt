ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating The Foundry (the "Venue"), and The Velvet Rivers ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on February 8, 2026, with a set of not less than 63 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Compensation
Artist shall receive 77.5% of Net Box Office Receipts. No guarantee applies to this Engagement.

3. Bonuses
The following bonuses apply, and every bonus level reached is payable: upon the sale of 750 paid tickets, a bonus of US $750.00; 1,000 or more paid tickets: $1,500.00.
Complimentary tickets do not count toward any bonus.

4. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than US $1,000,000.00 per occurrence.

5. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at Forty-Five Dollars (US $45.00) for GA, $80 for VIP and Forty-Five Dollars (US $45.00) for Balcony. A service charge of US $6.00 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

6. Allowable Expenses
All production and operating costs of the Engagement are borne by Promoter. No expenses shall be deducted from Artist's compensation.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of California. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Velvet Rivers
