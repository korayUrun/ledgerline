ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating Union Stage (the "Venue"), and The Golden Pines ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on May 7, 2026, with a set of not less than 87 minutes. Doors open at 8:00 p.m.

2. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 70% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 30% as a merchandise fee.

3. Payment to Artist
3.1 Promoter shall pay Artist the greater of (a) US $4,000.00 (the "Guarantee") or (b) 72.5% of net box office receipts remaining after Allowable Expenses have been deducted.
3.2 A deposit of Two Thousand Dollars (US $2,000.00) is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

4. Expenses
The following costs are Allowable Expenses and may be deducted before Artist's share is calculated: house staffing, catering, advertising, security, rented equipment and production costs.
Allowable catering expenses shall not exceed $500.
Allowable house staffing expenses shall not exceed $2,750.00.
In no event shall Allowable Expenses exceed Ten Thousand Dollars (US $10,000.00) in the aggregate.
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

5. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than One Million Dollars (US $1,000,000.00) per occurrence.

6. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at $30 for GA and US $60.00 for VIP. A service charge of $5 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Golden Pines
