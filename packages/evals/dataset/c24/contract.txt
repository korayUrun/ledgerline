ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating The Foundry (the "Venue"), and The Copper Harbors ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on July 27, 2026, with a set of not less than 71 minutes. Doors open at 7:00 p.m.

2. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than Two Million Dollars (US $2,000,000.00) per occurrence.

3. Compensation
3.1 Compensation is Six Thousand Dollars (US $6,000.00) (the "Guarantee") plus a back-end share: after Promoter has recovered Allowable Expenses, the Guarantee and a Promoter Profit equal to ten percent (10%) of Allowable Expenses plus the Guarantee, Artist shall receive 80% of all remaining net box office receipts.
3.2 A deposit of $3,000 is payable upon signing and will be credited against the amount due to Artist. The balance is payable on the night of the show by wire or company check.

4. Allowable Expenses
The following costs are Allowable Expenses and may be deducted before Artist's share is calculated: advertising, venue staff (ushers and box office), security, rented equipment, production costs and catering.
Security costs are capped at $1,250.00.
Each expense must be supported by a receipt presented at settlement.

5. Cancellation
If Promoter cancels the Engagement less than 27 days before the show date for any reason other than force majeure, Promoter shall pay Artist a cancellation fee of Two Thousand Dollars (US $2,000.00).

6. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at $30 for GA. A service charge of US $6.00 per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

7. Radius Clause
Artist shall not perform within 45 miles of the Venue during the 50 days before the show date.

8. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Copper Harbors
