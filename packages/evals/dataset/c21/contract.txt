ARTIST ENGAGEMENT AGREEMENT

This Artist Engagement Agreement (the "Agreement") is made between Lantern Hill Productions LLC ("Promoter"), operating Hall 9 (the "Venue"), and The Silver Owls ("Artist"). The terms below are synthetic test data.

1. Engagement
Artist shall perform one show at the Venue on February 27, 2026, with a set of not less than 76 minutes. Doors open at 8:00 p.m.

2. Insurance
Promoter shall maintain general liability insurance of not less than Two Million Dollars (US $2,000,000.00) per occurrence.

3. Allowable Expenses
Promoter is responsible for all show costs. Artist's compensation is not reduced by any expense.

4. Compensation
In full consideration of the performance, Promoter agrees to pay Artist a fixed fee of US $30,500.00 (the "Guarantee"). Artist shall not share in box office receipts.

5. Merchandise
Artist shall retain 90% of gross merchandise sales; the Venue shall retain the remaining 10% as a merchandise fee.

6. Ticketing
Tickets shall be priced at Twenty-Five Dollars (US $25.00) for GA. A service charge of Five Dollars (US $5.00) per ticket is paid by the buyer and is not part of gross receipts.

7. General Provisions
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Illinois. It is the entire agreement between the parties and may be changed only in writing signed by both parties.

For Promoter: Lantern Hill Productions LLC
For Artist: The Silver Owls
